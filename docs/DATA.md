# Data, figures and attribution

## Public demonstration data

`examples/make_demo_data.py` calculates a four-site periodic spin-1 chain:

H = sum_i (Sx_i Sx_(i+1) + Sy_i Sy_(i+1) + Jz Sz_i Sz_(i+1))
    + D sum_i (Sz_i)^2.

It diagonalizes the complete 81-dimensional Hamiltonian and averages expectation
values equally across eigenvectors within 1e-9 of the lowest eigenvalue.
Features `2`–`4`, `5`–`7`, `8`–`10` are Cx, Cy, Cz at distances 1–3 from site
zero. There are 12 points per sampled parameter regime, 36 in total. The
parameter table records Jz, D and degeneracy; parameters are not classifier
features. Labels 0/1/2 identify ferromagnetic-like, Haldane-like and large-D-like
parameter regimes and are not finite-size phase determinations.

The data are calculated locally, require no download, and are independent of
the historical research spreadsheets. The generating code and its outputs
are project-authored and available under the project's MIT terms. CSV outputs
are generated by the documented demo and are not committed.

## Selected historical findings

`selected_results/gains.csv` and `contrasts.csv` are exact extractions of two
rounded aggregate tables from the approved v3 handoff at the research revision
in `PUBLICATION.json`. The source report's SHA-256 is recorded there. These
are derived numerical findings, not copies of third-party source datasets.
The main research runs used code revision `e1f73c0` and the identifiers:

- `v3-gan-ed21-sem-anomalias-v1`
- `v3-ddpm-ed21-sem-anomalias-v1`
- `v3-ctrl-copias-ed21-sem-anomalias-v1`
- `v3-ctrl-centro_caixa-ed21-sem-anomalias-v1`
- `v3-ctrl-interpolacao-ed21-sem-anomalias-v1`
- `v3-ctrl-jitter-ed21-sem-anomalias-v1`

The two figures are newly rendered from these CSVs using
`scripts/plot_selected_results.py`. They inherit the rounding of the tables;
contrasts were calculated before rounding, so subtracting rounded mean gains
can differ by 0.01 pp. No new scientific run is implied. Figure sources are
the published CSVs; full checkpoints, per-unit runs and internal reports are
not part of this release. The public package reproduces the figures and demo,
but cannot independently recompute the main scientific grid from raw data.
